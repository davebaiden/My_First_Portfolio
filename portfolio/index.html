Image Recognition

I trained a machine learning model using Google’s Teachable Machine to recognize three hand gestures: thumbs-up, thumbs-down, and a neutral gesture (anything outside the two specified). Through collecting examples and iteratively refining the training data, I taught the model to accurately distinguish between positive, negative, and neutral gestures. This project sharpened my understanding of computer vision, model training, and real-time gesture detection, and was a fun way to explore practical applications of AI in interactive systems.
